Jobs in Maryland Government

Maryland's state government is one of the largest employers in the state, spanning everything from public safety to health care to transportation. Here's how the hiring process actually works.

Where openings are posted

All Maryland executive-branch state job openings are posted on the official state employment portal, jobs.maryland.gov. That's the single source of truth — third-party job boards sometimes mirror listings, but applications for state positions are ultimately submitted through the state's own system. County and municipal government jobs (a Baltimore City position, for example, or a county school system) are typically posted separately on that jurisdiction's own site rather than the state portal.

Common job categories

Health & human services

Nursing, social work, public health, and behavioral health roles across state hospitals and agencies.

Public safety

Corrections, state police, emergency management, and related support roles.

Transportation

MDOT, the MVA, and highway administration — engineering, maintenance, and administrative positions.

Administration & IT

Office administration, finance, HR, and technology roles supporting every state agency.

Education

State-level education administration (K-12 teaching jobs are hired by individual county school systems, not the state portal).

Natural resources & environment

Parks, wildlife, and environmental agencies, including seasonal park positions.

What to expect from the process

  • Create a candidate profile on jobs.maryland.gov before applying — most postings route through that account system.
  • Minimum qualifications are strict. State job postings list specific education and experience requirements; applications that don't clearly demonstrate meeting them are often screened out before a human review.
  • Veterans' preference applies to Maryland state hiring — eligible veterans should note this on their application per the portal's instructions.
  • Timelines run longer than private-sector hiring — postings often stay open for weeks, and the review/interview process can take a month or more after the closing date.
  • Pay follows a published state salary schedule tied to job grade rather than individual negotiation, so posted salary ranges are a reliable guide to what a role actually pays.

This page is independent guidance, not affiliated with the State of Maryland. For authoritative details on open positions, requirements, and how to apply, use jobs.maryland.gov directly.
