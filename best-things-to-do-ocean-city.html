Best Things to Do in Ocean City, Maryland

Ocean City offers a mix of beach relaxation, boardwalk entertainment, water sports, and casual dining. Whether you want an action-packed day or a quiet beach getaway, there's something for every traveler and every budget.

Main attractions by interest

Beach & water

Swimming, paddleboarding, kayaking, surfing, jet skiing, and fishing dominate the water-based side of town.

Boardwalk

Trimper's Rides, arcades, restaurants, ice cream shops, and the historic 1-mile wooden boardwalk.

Family activities

Mini golf, go-karts, water parks, arcade games, and casual attractions spread throughout town.

Dining & nightlife

Casual boardwalk food, sit-down seafood restaurants, bars, and nightlife concentrated near downtown.

Top-tier things to do

  • Spend a full day at the beach: arrive early, swim, relax with a book, and catch the sunset.
  • Walk the entire boardwalk: grab food, play arcade games, ride Trimper's Ferris wheel, and people-watch.
  • Try paddleboarding or kayaking: rent from a local shop and explore the calm bay waters.
  • Ride Trimper's historic roller coaster: a classic Ocean City experience and a bucket-list item for many visitors.
  • Fish from the pier: simple, low-cost, and relaxing for both kids and adults.
  • Explore neighborhoods beyond the boardwalk: Midtown and Uptown have quieter streets, local restaurants, and fewer crowds.
  • Check the current forecast and plan a beach weather day: avoid the hottest and most crowded times.

How to structure your day

Morning (8–11am): Arrive at the beach early when it is cooler and quieter. Swim and relax. Midday (11am–3pm): Grab lunch, head back to your hotel for a break or a pool time, and reapply sunscreen. Late afternoon (3–6pm): Return to the beach for another swim or start exploring the boardwalk. Evening (6pm+): Dinner on or near the boardwalk, arcade games, and rides until it gets dark.

Rainy day alternatives

If weather turns bad, head indoors: boardwalk arcades, indoor mini golf, seafood restaurants, and shopping malls are all solid options. Check the Ocean City forecast and plan a rainy-day backup.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do on my first day?

Arrive early, check into your hotel, and head straight to the beach. Swim, relax, and get oriented. Save the boardwalk for later or the next day.

What is the most popular activity?

Swimming and beach lounging are the main draws. The boardwalk comes in second for entertainment and dining.

How much time should I spend on the boardwalk?

A few hours is typical for a casual stroll and a meal. A full day or evening if you want to ride Trimper's and play arcades.

Is there anything to do off-season?

Yes, but many attractions have reduced hours or are closed. The beach and boardwalk are still walkable and worth visiting even in winter or early spring.

Activity availability and hours vary by season. Check individual attractions for current operating schedules and pricing.
