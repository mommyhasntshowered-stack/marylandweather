Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

What this site collects

Maryland Weather & Guide does not require an account, does not collect names or email addresses through the site itself, and does not sell personal data. Weather requests you trigger (selecting a city, loading a forecast) are sent directly from your browser to our weather data providers — Open-Meteo and the National Weather Service — to fetch the forecast you asked for.

Advertising & cookies

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technology to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites on the internet. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and manage or opt out of personalized advertising, at:

  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services
  • Google Ads Settings
  • aboutads.info opt-out (US)
  • youronlinechoices.eu (EU)

Third-party data sources

Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo and the National Weather Service. Each provider's own terms and privacy practices apply to requests made to their APIs from your browser.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time; the date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
