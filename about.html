About Maryland Weather & Guide

A single, fast place for the everyday questions people have about Maryland: what's the weather doing, where things are, what time it is, and what to expect on a trip to the coast.

This site pulls live weather data from the Open-Meteo API and active weather alerts from the National Weather Service, refreshed on every page load. The travel and geography guides — Ocean City, the Queenstown outlets, Maryland's counties, state government hiring — are written and maintained independently.
