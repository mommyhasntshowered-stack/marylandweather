Hotels in Ocean City, Maryland

Ocean City runs about 10 miles north to south, and where you stay changes the whole trip — here's how the town breaks down and what to weigh before you book.

Where to stay: OC's neighborhoods

Downtown / Boardwalk (S. Division–27th St)

The classic OC stay — walkable to Trimper's, the arcades, and the Inlet. Older, mostly independent motels and hotels. Busiest and loudest part of town, best for boardwalk-first trips.

Midtown (28th–75th St)

The middle ground — a mix of hotels, condos, and family resorts, close to the convention center and most restaurants. A short drive or bike ride to the boardwalk.

Uptown (76th–130th+ St)

Newer, quieter, and more spread out, with wider beaches and bigger resort-style hotels. Best if you want distance from the boardwalk crowds and don't mind driving.

Bayside

Motels and hotels along the bay side of Coastal Highway are usually cheaper than oceanfront, often with pools, and a short walk across the highway to the beach.

Oceanfront vs. bayside vs. a few blocks back

Oceanfront rooms cost the most and book up first for summer weekends, but you're paying for the view and direct beach access. Rooms one or two blocks back — "ocean view" or "partial view" listings — are often noticeably cheaper for a two-minute walk to the sand. Bayside hotels trade beach proximity for lower rates and, often, a pool and easier parking.

When to book

  • June–August weekends: book as early as possible — rooms in prime boardwalk locations sell out months ahead for peak summer.
  • September: the best value-to-weather ratio — warm water, thinner crowds, lower rates.
  • Off-season (Oct–April): heavy discounts, but many boardwalk businesses close for the winter — check what's open before you go.
  • Condos vs. hotels: weekly condo rentals (common on the Eastern Shore) usually beat nightly hotel rates for stays of 5+ nights, especially for groups.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the best area to stay in Ocean City?

It depends on your trip. Boardwalk-area hotels are best for convenience and nightlife, while Midtown and Uptown are better for quieter family stays and more space.

Is it cheaper to stay bayside or oceanfront?

Bayside hotels are usually less expensive, especially outside peak summer weekends. Oceanfront rooms are pricier but offer direct beach access and the classic Ocean City view.

When is the best time to book a hotel?

Book early for June through August weekends. September is often the sweet spot for better rates and still-warm water, while off-season stays can be much cheaper.

Should I stay near the boardwalk or farther north?

If you want to walk to arcades, restaurants, and the beach, choose downtown or midtown. If you want more room and a calmer feel, Uptown is usually the better choice.
