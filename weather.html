Maryland weather at a glance

Maryland's weather varies more than its small size suggests. The Atlantic coast around Ocean City runs milder and windier than the rest of the state, tempered by the ocean in both summer and winter. Central Maryland — Baltimore, Annapolis, and the D.C. suburbs — sees a classic mid-Atlantic mix: humid summers in the 80s and 90s°F, crisp falls, and winters that swing between mild rain and sudden snow. Out west, the mountains around Cumberland and Garrett County run several degrees cooler year-round and pick up the state's heaviest snowfall.
