Best Hotels in Ocean City, Maryland

Ocean City is one of Maryland's most popular summer destinations, but the best stay depends on what kind of trip you're planning. Some visitors want to walk to the boardwalk, while others want a quieter beach stay or a family-friendly resort with a pool.

Best areas to stay in Ocean City

Downtown / Boardwalk

Best for travelers who want restaurants, arcades, and beach access all within walking distance. This area is busiest and loudest, but it is ideal if your goal is to be in the middle of the action.

Midtown

A strong middle ground. Midtown gives you a mix of family hotels, condo-style stays, and easier access to the beach without the heaviest downtown crowds.

Uptown

Best for travelers who want more space, a quieter beach experience, and resort-style properties farther from the boardwalk.

Bayside

Often a better value if you are trying to cut costs. Bayside hotels may be a short drive or walk to the beach, but they usually offer lower nightly rates and good parking options.

Best hotels by traveler type

If you want the easiest answer, the best place to stay depends on the kind of trip you are planning:

  • Best for first-time visitors: stay in the boardwalk or downtown area if you want to be near the action.
  • Best for families: choose Midtown or a resort-style hotel with a pool and easier parking.
  • Best for couples: look at quieter oceanfront or uptown stays with a more relaxed beach feel.
  • Best for lower prices: bayside properties or stays a few blocks back from the beach can be much less expensive in peak season.

How to choose the right Ocean City hotel

Before booking, ask how much time you want to spend walking, driving, and dealing with crowds. Oceanfront rooms are convenient and scenic, but they often cost the most. A hotel just a block or two off the beach can save money while still giving you easy access to the sand. A hotel farther north may feel calmer and less hectic, especially if you are traveling with children or want a more relaxed vacation.

Best time to book Ocean City hotels

Summer weekends book first, especially in the most in-demand boardwalk areas. If you can travel in late August or September, you often get better rates and still warm water. Shoulder season can be a great choice if you want a calmer trip without paying peak-season prices.

  • June to August: book early, especially for beach-front rooms and weekend stays.
  • September: one of the best value periods for a beach trip.
  • Off-season: more affordable rates, but some businesses and attractions may have reduced hours.

Popular travel questions

Where is the best place to stay in Ocean City?

It depends on your trip. Downtown and the boardwalk are best for convenience; Midtown is great for families; Uptown is better for a quieter, more spread-out beach stay.

Is it worth paying for an oceanfront hotel?

Usually yes if beach access and views are a priority. If you are trying to save money, a hotel one or two blocks back can still be very close to the sand and noticeably cheaper.

Which hotels are best for families?

Look for hotels with pools, family-friendly layouts, and easy parking in Midtown or slightly north of the busiest boardwalk area.

When should I reserve a room?

For summer weekends, book as early as possible. If you are flexible, September often gives the best combination of weather and lower rates.
